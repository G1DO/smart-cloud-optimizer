# IEEE-Style Title Options

Ranked by defensibility for the current evidence.

1. **Operationalizing Capacity-Reservation Theory for Cloud Cost Optimization Under Demand Surges**
   - Most defensible because it emphasizes the theory-practice bridge and does
     not sound like a product demo.

2. **When P95 Right-Sizing Meets Reserved Capacity: A Reproducible Study of Cloud Cost Optimization**
   - Strong and memorable; foregrounds the main empirical tension.

3. **Measuring the Price of Conservatism in Cloud Right-Sizing and Reservation Policies**
   - Best if the paper is shortened around Study III and the policy simulation.

4. **A Reproducible Evaluation of Conservative Cloud Right-Sizing Under Intermittent Demand Surges**
   - Conservative systems-measurement title; less explicit about reservation
     theory.

5. **From FinOps Heuristics to Capacity-Reservation Policy: Evidence from a Cloud Cost-Optimization Pipeline**
   - Broadest framing; useful if the venue values system operationalization.
